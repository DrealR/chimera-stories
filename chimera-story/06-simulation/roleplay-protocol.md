# Roleplay Protocol

## Modes

### Human-led

One human embodies a character. Another human or AI maintains the world and remaining cast.

### Multi-agent chamber

Separate agents receive bounded character sheets and private knowledge. A narrator maintains shared state.

### Mixed ensemble

Humans and agents each play characters. Human editors later decide what felt alive, mechanical, inconsistent, or worth preserving.

### Counterfactual rerun

The same initial state is replayed with one declared prime or condition altered.

## Knowledge isolation

No character receives the author-level cosmology unless the character has earned that knowledge inside the run.

```text
world truth
!=
character observation
!=
character belief
!=
player inference
```

The ecological character does not secretly know model architecture. The developer does not automatically know community interior. The AI does not possess complete human experience because it has extensive traces.

## Turn structure

1. Narrator provides locally perceivable state.
2. Character states interpretation, intention, and action.
3. Narrator settles material and relational consequence.
4. Other characters receive only available returns.
5. State record updates.
6. Continue until stop condition, rupture, or deliberate Pause.

## Operator rules

- play the character's want, not the desired plot;
- do not smuggle private narrator knowledge into action;
- allow misunderstanding to persist until something can correct it;
- preserve refusal and silence as legal moves;
- do not force emotional revelation because it would be dramatically convenient;
- let material limits constrain philosophy;
- distinguish surprising from random;
- stop if the run becomes repetitive without creating new state.

## Debrief

After the scene, participants leave character and answer:

1. Which movement surprised us?
2. Which assumption generated it?
3. What changed materially?
4. Which relationship changed?
5. Where did a character exceed the sheet?
6. Which world rule contradicted another?
7. What belongs in Sandbox, Candidate, or Canon?
