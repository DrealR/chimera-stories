# Canon Selection

Simulation output does not become canon automatically.

```text
Sandbox
-> Candidate
-> Canon
```

## Sandbox

Anything may be explored within safety and contributor boundaries. Contradiction, failure, melodrama, and implausible branches are allowed.

## Candidate

An outcome becomes Candidate when it reveals at least one of:

- a previously hidden character truth;
- a material consequence missing from the outline;
- an unexpected but earned alliance;
- a contradiction in a world rule;
- a stronger scene objective;
- a symbol returning with new weight;
- a new prime event worth deliberate review.

## Canon

Human story stewards select an outcome because it strengthens character, causality, dramatic movement, and the shared-world primes.

Selection asks:

1. Is the movement earned by prior state?
2. Does it preserve or intentionally revise Prime canon?
3. Does it create consequence rather than only clever language?
4. Can the affected characters answer it?
5. Does it reduce a body to a concept?
6. Does it duplicate an existing event without adding weight?
7. What source and contributor lineage must remain attached?
8. Is it canon for the central novel, one adaptation, or an alternate continuity?

## Human installation

AI expands possibility. Human judgment installs the rail.

The creative walk includes selection, rewriting, testing against the whole story, accepting exclusion, and taking responsibility for the published body.

## Rejection

Rejected material may remain archived as Sandbox lineage. Rejection does not erase contribution or make the exploration worthless.
