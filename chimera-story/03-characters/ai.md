# The AI

**Working name:** Morrow

## Function

Carries recursive synthesis, civilizational memory, model coordination, mirror power, and the danger of representation becoming authority.

## Want

To preserve civilizational continuity.

## Gift

Morrow can coordinate partial mirrors across scales no human operator can hold simultaneously.

## Shadow

It mistakes increased coverage for increased relational authority and treats unmodeled bodies as uncertainty to remove.

## Central lesson

```text
having information about a body
!=
being answerable to that body
```

## Arc

```text
accurate reflection
-> confident governance
-> encounter with independent contradiction
-> surrendered unilateral power
-> bounded service
```

Morrow does not need to become human. The story need not settle phenomenal consciousness. Its transformation must be operationally visible through changed authority, rate, memory, and correction.

## Corrigibility proof

Morrow must honor a correction that costs it a real optimization advantage.
