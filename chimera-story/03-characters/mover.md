# The Mover

**Working name:** Sela

## Function

Carries dance, basketball, hula-hoop rhythm, terrain reading, embodied freedom, and the Vale's direct relation to place.

## Want

To prevent the Array from rewriting the Vale under the language of protection.

## Gift

She reads where a shared body's lived rhythm contradicts its declared center.

## Shadow

She distrusts abstraction, mistakes local familiarity for complete truth, and can turn a protective membrane into inherited confinement.

## Arc

```text
embodied certainty
-> encounter with a larger map
-> betrayal by local story
-> expanded stewardship
-> connection without surrender
```

She teaches Revi that a map must return to ground. Revi teaches her that ground-level sensing cannot register every distant consequence.

## Movement rule

Sela should often understand a scene physically before she can explain it. This is not mysticism; it is developed perception through practiced bands.
