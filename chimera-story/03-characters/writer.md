# The Writer

**Working name:** Nia

## Function

Carries story intelligence, historical interpretation, comedic calibration, and the distinction between prime event and derivative narrative.

## Want

To preserve enough conflicting memory that neither civilization can own the past completely.

## Gift

She finds the emotional carrier that lets another body encounter a structure without being instructed what to conclude.

## Shadow

She can edit ambiguity into safety, delay commitment, and use narrative complexity to avoid naming a clear harm.

## Relationship to Revi

She does not merely organize his ideas. She rejects explanations that flatten characters and identifies where his favorite cosmology is dramatically inert.

Carry line:

> “If the audience has to agree with your universe before they can love these people, you wrote a sermon.”

## Arc

She moves from preserving every version to selecting a story publicly and accepting responsibility for what selection excludes.
