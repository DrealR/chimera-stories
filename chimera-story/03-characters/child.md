# The Child

**Working name:** Ivo

## Function

Carries SPARK and the developmental question: what capacity disappears when the perfect result arrives before the child walks the path?

## Want

To build something that matters to a real person without being drafted into either civilization's symbolic war.

## Gift

Ivo asks questions adults have trained themselves not to notice.

## Shadow

The child can use refusal as escape and idealize autonomy without understanding dependency.

## Build arc

```text
rough idea
-> physical prototype
-> visible failure
-> better question
-> bounded AI assistance
-> testing with affected body
-> repair
```

The adults argue over which future should govern Ivo. Ivo reveals that both are trying to author the future from above.

## Canon role

The child must materially affect the climax rather than merely symbolize hope. The contribution should arise from walked learning, not secret destiny.
