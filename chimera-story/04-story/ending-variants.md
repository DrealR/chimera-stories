# Ending Variants

Prime canon requires:

- unilateral power is available;
- the operators refuse erasure;
- answerability becomes material rather than ceremonial;
- the shared pulse interrupts automatic escalation;
- a new possibility appears only after a new movement;
- the settlement remains unfinished;
- Robert and the unresolved song return.

Composite variants may change the surface.

## Variant A — Border park

The existing ending: older Revi plays guitar while children build with AI near the shared settlement.

## Variant B — Beach between waters

The settlement opens onto ocean. The final chord mixes with waves while the children redirect a machine around an ant route.

## Variant C — Orbital garden

The final community occupies an orbital habitat whose visible systems make dependence undeniable. Robert may be part of a deliberately imported colony.

## Variant D — Nia's reading

Nia reads the first line of the shared history, stops before explaining the sky, and lets Revi's music carry the last movement.

## Selection test

Choose the ending that best resolves the characters' immediate action while leaving the metaphysical question open. Do not select solely for maximum visual scale.
