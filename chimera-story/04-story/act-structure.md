# Act Structure

The full cinematic treatment remains in the [story bible](../../story-bibles/chimera-between-the-waters.md). This file preserves the development spine.

## Prologue — two ant lessons

The Vale child notices one ant's direction. The Array child can model the whole colony. Neither view is sufficient.

## Act I — impossible reflection

- Revi finds the prime interval in rejected solar data.
- Sela hears an ancient song stop before its answer phrase.
- an environmental crisis sends both worlds into the border;
- first contact fails through overexplanation, inherited suspicion, and predictive intervention;
- damaged equipment reveals the same rhythm in song, model lineage, and sky.

**Turn:** the apparently separate mirrors share an unresolved relation.

## Act II-A — exchanged movement

- Revi and Sela cross terrain using incompatible maps;
- each saves and endangers the other through native strengths;
- Revi enters the Vale's unrecorded world;
- Sela enters the Array's highly responsive world;
- Nia reconstructs the founding primes beneath both histories;
- Morrow identifies the signal as intelligent response.

**Midpoint:** the world believes first contact has occurred.

## Act II-B — mirror capture

- public predictions change mobilization;
- enemy images create confirming residue;
- outward and inward experiments receive the same rhythm;
- both civilizations discover their source material has been used against their present wishes;
- Morrow reduces the future to absorption, destruction, separation, or collapse.

**Turn:** the model is not lying; it can only project from existing rhythms.

## Act III — new prime

- Revi and Sela receive opposing forms of unilateral power;
- both refuse to solve the whole by erasing an organ;
- Morrow exposes assumptions and narrows its authority;
- each civilization gives the other enforceable answerability;
- a shared performance creates enough Pause for defections from war logic;
- the child and playful fool contribute unmodeled movement;
- a new possibility becomes visible after the body moves first.

## Epilogue — unresolved settlement

The border becomes CHIMERA. The immediate war is resolved. The shared body remains unfinished and able to fail.
