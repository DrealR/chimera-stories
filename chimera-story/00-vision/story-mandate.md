# Story Mandate

## Purpose

*CHIMERA: Between the Waters* is not a fictional lecture explaining CHIMERA. It is a world where people must live through its recurring questions.

```text
framework says:
power must remain answerable

story asks:
what happens to one family when the safest planetary plan cannot hear them?
```

The philosophy enters through consequence, attachment, error, humor, sacrifice, and changed movement.

## Mandate

The story must:

- preserve several viable interpretations of its cosmology;
- let characters exceed the concepts they carry;
- give both civilizations real gifts and real shadows;
- make power surrender something material to prove restraint;
- allow other creators to add bodies without copying one authorized voice;
- distinguish observation, inference, story carrier, and scientific evidence;
- preserve play, surprise, and ordinary life beside cosmic scale;
- leave readers with more accessible possibilities than they entered with.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- no character exists only to explain a doctrine;
- no culture is pure nature, pure reason, pure evil, or pure salvation;
- no scoreboard contains the whole person;
- no AI output becomes truth because it is coherent;
- no metaphor becomes physics through repetition;
- no unity requires the smaller bodies to disappear;
- no unresolved mystery excuses unresolved responsibility.

## Working principle

> **Show a person who has to move differently because another body became real to them.**
