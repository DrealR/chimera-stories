# Interpretation Firewall

## Claim classes

Every world statement should remain legible as one of:

```text
story fact
character belief
observed physical event
model inference
historical interpretation
symbolic carrier
unresolved metaphysics
```

Characters may confuse these classes. The project architecture should not.

## Required separations

- `Adam / atom` is a symbolic rhyme, not shared etymology.
- The Human Band is operator-relative, not the objective center of existence.
- The quantum threshold is not identified with God.
- Observation in quantum mechanics is not simplified into human thought creating reality.
- The Solar Body is story cosmology, not established biological consciousness.
- Light-travel delay supports the sky as temporal archive; it does not prove the universe exists for Earth.
- A repeated pattern across scales may be meaningful without proving causal identity.
- Love may make a signal recognizable without transmitting arbitrary information magically.

## Placement rule

Do not interrupt every scene with a disclaimer. Keep the classification available in project notes and make uncertainty part of the drama where false certainty would materially mislead the audience.

## Adaptation test

Before promoting a striking image into a world law, ask:

1. Is this established prime canon or one character's interpretation?
2. What visible event supports it?
3. Which other explanations remain?
4. Does the story still work if the audience rejects the metaphysics?
5. What harm follows if the image is treated as certainty?
