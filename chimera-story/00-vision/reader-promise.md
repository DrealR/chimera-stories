# Reader Promise

The central novel must remain complete for a reader who never opens the framework, simulation files, source notes, or adaptation archive.

The reader is promised:

- characters with wants larger than the concepts assigned to them;
- a discoverable mystery rather than a pre-solved cosmology;
- emotional consequences for abstract systems;
- moments of humor, play, quiet, and bodily life;
- ambiguity that invites interpretation without hiding missing plot logic;
- an ending that resolves the immediate moral action while preserving the world's future.

The reader is not required to believe:

- the cosmos literally mirrors Earth;
- quantum physics proves a theology;
- AI possesses human interiority;
- CHIMERA is the final account of reality;
- every structural rhyme shares a mechanism.

Different successful readings may emphasize romance, political conflict, AI governance, ecological memory, theology, music, games, the child, or Robert the ant.

> **The book gives the reader enough structure to form a world and enough absence to make that world partly theirs.**
