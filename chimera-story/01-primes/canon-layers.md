# Prime Canon and Composite Canon

## Prime canon

Prime canon contains the minimum stable relations and events from which the shared world derives.

A prime is stable at the declared story scale. It may remain mysterious internally.

```text
prime canon asks:
what must survive for this to remain Between the Waters?
```

## Composite canon

Composite canon is one authored arrangement of names, scenes, chronology, relationships, images, and explanations built from the primes.

```text
composite canon asks:
which configuration does this novel or adaptation select?
```

Names, romances, visual designs, faction order, and exact mechanisms can change while the underlying relational problem survives.

## Canon states

| State | Meaning | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Sandbox | unrestricted exploration | none |
| Candidate | outcome worth preserving for review | provisional |
| Composite canon | selected for one work or continuity | local |
| Prime canon | irreducible shared-world anchor | cross-adaptation |
| Retired | once selected, no longer active | historical only |

## Prime-change rule

Prime canon can change, but only through an explicit revision record that states:

- which prime changed;
- why the old form constrained the living story;
- what adaptations it affects;
- which prior works remain valid in their own continuity;
- who participated in the decision.

Prime canon is a continuity carrier, not an ownership weapon.
