# Relational Primes

These relations organize the story beneath its costumes:

- another body's reality can redirect movement without erasing either center;
- visibility, access, understanding, and ownership remain distinct;
- power must remain answerable to the bodies it affects;
- a representation can guide a body without containing it;
- a useful mirror can become a governing prison;
- shared rhythm can coordinate difference without imposing identical movement;
- local protection can export harm into a larger body;
- a future remains alive only while correction and refusal remain possible;
- love permits relation while preserving distinct centers;
- play can reveal a movement that optimization cannot generate from its existing score.

The climax must instantiate these relations through action. A speech alone cannot satisfy them.
