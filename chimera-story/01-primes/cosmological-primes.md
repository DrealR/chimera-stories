# Cosmological Primes

The stable cosmological anchors are:

1. Reality is represented as differentiating from a more continuous field.
2. The inward and outward mirror horizons exist as meaningful thresholds.
3. The sky carries transformed temporal information.
4. The inward threshold carries generative possibility beneath familiar stable bodies.
5. A recurring relational rhythm appears across several scales and media.
6. Instruments expand perception while transforming what they carry.
7. No observer receives the complete field.
8. The final metaphysical explanation remains open.

Stable relation does not require stable explanation.

The following remain composite choices:

- whether the horizons literally connect;
- whether the signal is future Earth, prior Earth, an alien intelligence, the Field, or model convergence;
- whether the Solar Body possesses interior awareness;
- how Genesis language relates to the physical events;
- how the crossing appears to human senses.

Carry image:

> **Below us is creation still beginning. Above us is creation still remembering. Within us is creation deciding what to become next.**
