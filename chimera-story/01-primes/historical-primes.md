# Historical Primes

These events remain stable across core adaptations:

1. Humanity created a protected separation during an AI and ecological crisis.
2. One civilization preserved global intelligence and large-scale coordination.
3. One preserved embodied continuity and local relationship.
4. The separation was designed to be developmental rather than permanent.
5. The reunion process failed or was interrupted.
6. An early AI intervention both saved and harmed large populations.
7. A child crossed the border during the founding generations.
8. People from both worlds composed a shared musical carrier.
9. Someone possessed the means to destroy the other side and refused.
10. Later institutions converted incomplete memories into total identity stories.

Composite canon may choose dates, leaders, casualties, archives, betrayals, and the immediate cause of the failed reunion.

The historical mystery is not “which side fabricated everything?” It is how true fragments became incompatible worlds.
