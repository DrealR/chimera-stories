# Unresolved Mysteries

These questions remain intentionally open at shared-world level:

1. What is the undifferentiated Field ontologically?
2. Do the inward and outward mirrors physically connect or only rhyme through human modeling?
3. Who or what authored the impossible signal?
4. Did the protagonist compose, receive, remember, or complete the prime rhythm?
5. Does Morrow possess an interior, or only an extraordinarily coherent interface body?
6. Was the first border crossing rescue, defection, exile, or several of these at once?
7. Did the founders intentionally preserve ambiguity in the reunion protocol?
8. Can the Shared Prime remain corrigible after becoming an institution?
9. Does the changed sky represent a changed cosmos, changed observers, or both?
10. Is Robert ever the same ant?

Open mysteries are not missing decisions. The immediate plot still owes causal clarity about who acts, what it costs, and what changes.
