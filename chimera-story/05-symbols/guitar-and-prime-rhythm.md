# Guitar and the Prime Rhythm

Guitar is:

- an atmospheric organ;
- proof of embodied traversal;
- a recursive mirror of touch, sound, hearing, and correction;
- a bridge between mathematical relation and lived interior;
- a medium for shared pulse without identical parts.

The prime rhythm is:

```text
difference
-> approach
-> contact
-> separation
-> return with variation
```

Its musical expression may change across adaptations. The invariant is relational, not one copyrighted melody.

The final chord remains unresolved because present responsibility has been met while future relation remains open.

Use limit: music creates coordination conditions. It does not remove coercion, material conflict, or the need for explicit political action.
