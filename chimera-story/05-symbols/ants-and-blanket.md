# Ants and the Blanket

## Ant

The ant is a complete local body easily reduced to colony statistic, background movement, nuisance, or symbol.

Naming one “Robert” does not establish identity. It changes the observer's attention enough to notice direction and consequence.

```text
uncertainty without indifference
story without false certainty
attention without ownership
```

## Blanket

The blanket is comfort for one body and pathway obstruction for another.

It carries local optimization without moral melodrama:

```text
I create a place to rest
-> another body's route changes
-> noticing allows a light correction
```

## Use limit

Do not turn ants into a claim that individual humans are insignificant or that colony logic justifies authoritarian coordination.
