# The Sky Mirror

The sky carries several functions:

- physically delayed light;
- archive of earlier states;
- instrument-extended field;
- cultural projection surface;
- navigation system;
- apparent possibility mirror inside the fiction.

The story uses the sky to show:

```text
past signal
-> present interpretation
-> future movement
```

The mirror becomes dangerous when its representation is treated as destiny and commissions the future it predicted.

Use limit: the sky does not normally reflect ancient Earth directly back to Earth. Temporal-mirror language begins from light delay and expands into acknowledged fiction.
