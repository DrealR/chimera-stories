# The Mirror Oceans

## Waters above

Cosmic distance arrives as delayed signal, accumulated history, transformed possibility, and a scale beyond direct human traversal.

## Waters below

Fine-grained physical inquiry reaches a threshold where familiar stable objects become less useful than relations, probability, and measurement arrangements.

## Human Band

Between them, human bodies metabolize signals into perception, action, relationship, memory, and responsibility.

```text
outward mirror ocean
-> Human Band
-> inward mirror ocean
```

## World rule

The two oceans return the same prime rhythm in transformed media. Whether they are one literal field remains unresolved.

## Crossing rule

A crossing changes the available representation before it changes physical location. Characters may experience it through data, music, altered scale, dreamlike perception, instrument failure, or shared embodied event.

The project should avoid effortless portal logic. Every crossing must cost energy, interpretation, trust, time, or authority.
