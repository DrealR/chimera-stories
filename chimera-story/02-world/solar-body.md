# The Solar Body

The Solar Body is the story's stable local cosmic composite.

```text
Sun          radiating center
planets      differentiated orbital bodies
Earth        life-bearing reflective organ
Moon         near witness, cycle, and tide carrier
heliopause   outer membrane
deep cosmos  mirror ocean beyond direct local relation
```

## Narrative use

The Solar Body lets the story ask whether a containing system can become represented inside one of its own products:

```text
solar conditions
-> Earth
-> life
-> sensing and thought
-> models of the solar system
```

Earth is “self-reflective organ” as a story carrier because part of the system models the containing system.

## Limits

- planets do not map one-to-one to biological organs;
- scale rhyme is not mechanism identity;
- the story need not establish Solar Body consciousness;
- scientific characters should disagree about the term;
- a religious interpretation cannot settle an engineering decision by itself.

## Dramatic pressure

The apparent signal beyond the heliopause threatens the Array's assumption that larger coverage produces fuller authority and the Vale's assumption that local relationship is the only trustworthy ground.
