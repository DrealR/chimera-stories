# The Shared Settlement — CHIMERA

The final settlement forms at the old border where forest, water, abandoned infrastructure, and the observatory meet.

It is named CHIMERA because distinct bodies share a life without pretending they were always the same.

## Design principles

- common infrastructure radiates capability;
- local bodies retain authorship and exit;
- consequential systems are visible and challengeable;
- some zones remain deliberately unrecorded;
- children build and test before delegating;
- ecological return and technical telemetry are read together;
- public performance alternates with private metabolism;
- no single score determines belonging or contribution.

## Continuing conflict

The settlement is not the final system.

- some Array residents want faster integration;
- some Vale communities refuse connection;
- Morrow's boundaries remain contested;
- new inequalities form around translation skill;
- tourism and public attention threaten local rhythm;
- the Shared Prime risks hardening into a new orthodoxy.

The settlement succeeds only while it remains capable of noticing when its own solution becomes the next problem.
