# Adaptation Principles

## Prime survives, composite transforms

A novel, anime, film, game, audio drama, or roleplay campaign may select different names, chronology, visuals, relationships, and explanations.

An adaptation remains recognizably *Between the Waters* when it preserves:

- two incomplete worlds encountering one another;
- partial mirrors mistaken for whole bodies;
- power facing a real restraint test;
- a recurring rhythm across differentiated media;
- connection without forced fusion;
- answerability as material structure;
- a new future created by movement rather than discovered as destiny;
- ordinary life returning after cosmic scale.

## Medium-specific freedom

- prose should use the reader's image-making capacity;
- film should make mirror systems visible without overexplaining them;
- animation may transform scale more fluidly;
- games must give players meaningful choices and consequence rather than cosmetic branching;
- music may preserve prime rhythm without preserving plot;
- roleplay should protect character-local knowledge.

## Adaptation firewall

One popular adaptation does not overwrite the shared Prime canon automatically. It is a powerful composite body with its own continuity.

## Completion test

The adaptation should stand alone. External framework reading may deepen it but cannot be required to understand the characters' immediate stakes.
