# Provenance and Credit

The world is designed to generate other bodies. Generativity increases the need for lineage, not the right to erase it.

## Provenance record

For every retained contribution, preserve:

```yaml
artifact_id: ""
title: ""
creator_names: []
creation_date: ""
source_bodies: []
ai_systems_used: []
human_selection_and_revision: ""
canon_state: "sandbox | candidate | composite | prime | retired"
continuity: ""
license_or_permission: ""
credit_request: ""
derived_artifacts: []
```

## Credit principles

- influence and authorship are not identical;
- generation does not erase source lineage;
- editing and selection are creative labor;
- an AI system does not automatically become co-author because it produced candidates;
- private conversation does not become public story substrate without permission;
- alternate interpretations should cite the prime or composite bodies they transform;
- contributors may leave while their agreed contribution remains, subject to the permission attached at entry;
- disputes require visible revision history rather than silent rewriting.

## Shared-world posture

The project is a seed, not a monument. Open contribution does not mean public ownership of every source body. Selective permeability, consent, attribution, and versioned continuity allow the world to grow without consuming its creators.
