# Contributor Guide

## Invitation

Contributors may enter through character, scene, music, art, world design, simulation, criticism, research, or adaptation.

No contributor must reproduce CHIMERA vocabulary to participate.

## Before contributing

Read:

1. [Story Mandate](../00-vision/story-mandate.md)
2. [Canon Layers](../01-primes/canon-layers.md)
3. [Interpretation Firewall](../00-vision/interpretation-firewall.md)
4. the relevant character or world file

## Contribution note

Every proposed body should state:

- author or operator;
- date;
- source materials used;
- canon state;
- which primes it preserves or challenges;
- whether AI contributed and how;
- unresolved questions;
- requested form of credit.

## Creative freedom

Contributors may:

- reinterpret composite canon;
- introduce new local bodies;
- challenge character beliefs;
- propose alternate continuities;
- reject the literal cosmology;
- create work that emphasizes humor, romance, politics, music, or mystery.

Contributors may not silently:

- convert Sandbox material into shared canon;
- erase another contributor's lineage;
- use story ambiguity to promote unsafe factual claims;
- make the shared world permanently closed to correction;
- claim ownership of another person's private developmental history.
